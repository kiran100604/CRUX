What changed & why

1

Search-first, by meaning

A knowledge base’s natural verb is find. Big search wired to your real hybrid retrieval, not a substring filter.

was: tiny “Filter…” box doing substring match
2

One default view: related clusters

Things about the same topic sit together automatically. The 3-way tier/domain/topic toggle becomes a single “Grouped by ▾” for power users.

was: user must choose By tier / By domain / By topic up front
3

Plain language

“About / Level / Tags” instead of “domain / tier / altitude / leaf.” No vocabulary to learn before you can read your own knowledge.

was: Core Strategy · Leaf / Operational · Lens · altitude
4

Confidence: provenance + reasoning

“Where this came from” is now first, and every auto-classification shows why — so the user trusts that the right thing is in the right place.

was: source buried at bottom; classification unexplained
5

Control: fix the filing inline

About / Level / Tags are editable chips right on the card. One wrong auto-guess is a one-click fix, not a dead end.

was: tier/domain not editable at all
6

Less effort: connections are visible

Related facts surface in the detail as a “Related” list instead of waiting to be clicked or manually linked.

was: “see also” loaded passively, one at a time
